LlmentZero Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 16, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use LlmentZero, including people with disabilities and people who use assistive technology.

Plain-language summary

  • We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA across the website.
  • Every page is tested with automated tools on desktop and phone sizes.
  • Any content, including skill documents, is available in another format on request, at no extra cost.
  • Tell us about any barrier at llmentzero@gmail.com.

This summary is for convenience only. If it differs from the full text below, the full text applies.

1. Our commitment

LlmentZero is committed to providing a website and products that are accessible to everyone, and to treating people with disabilities with dignity and equal opportunity, in keeping with the principles of Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA).

2. Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

3. What we do

  • Test every page with automated accessibility tools on desktop and phone screen sizes before publishing changes.
  • Support full keyboard navigation, with visible focus indicators and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Use semantic headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can navigate the content.
  • Use text and colour combinations that meet WCAG contrast requirements.
  • Stop the decorative animated background when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Keep the main content usable with JavaScript turned off.
  • Provide skill documents as plain-text Markdown, which works with screen readers and can be converted to other formats.

4. Third-party services

Checkout is provided by Payhip, and sign-up and sign-in forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check for bots, which is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 and does not require puzzles. We do not control the accessibility of third-party services, but if you have trouble creating an account, signing in, buying or downloading, email us and we will help you complete your purchase another way.

5. Known limitations

Automated tools cannot catch every barrier, and the Site has not yet been tested by people who use assistive technology. We will continue to review and improve it.

6. Feedback and alternative formats

Email llmentzero@gmail.com to report a barrier or to request any content, including a skill document, in an alternative format or with communication support, at no extra cost. Please tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to respond within 5 business days and to provide alternative formats in a timely manner.

7. Changes

We will update this statement as the Site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.
