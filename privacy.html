LlmentZero Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what personal information LlmentZero collects, why, how it is protected, and the choices you have, in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Plain-language summary

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. The only cookies are strictly necessary sign-in cookies for your account.
  • For your free account we keep your email, a record of your agreement to our terms, your downloads and a keyed hash of your IP address for security.
  • We also collect what you send us by email and what Payhip shares with us when you buy.
  • We use it to answer you, deliver purchases, manage licences and keep required records. We never sell it.
  • Some service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States.
  • You can ask to see, correct or withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

This summary is for convenience only. If it differs from the full text below, the full text applies.

1. Who we are and accountability

LlmentZero is a sole proprietorship carrying on business in Ontario, Canada ("LlmentZero", "we", "us"). We are responsible for personal information under our control. Our privacy contact can be reached at llmentzero@gmail.com.

2. Definitions

"Personal information" means information about an identifiable individual, as defined in PIPEDA. "Service providers" means the third parties listed in section 6 that handle personal information on our behalf.

3. What we collect, and why

3.1 When you visit the website

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. The Site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you sign in, we set one strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in (see section 3.4), and, while you are entering a sign-in code, a second strictly necessary cookie that lasts 10 minutes and links the code to your browser. We do not use them for any other purpose. Your browser also keeps two small notes in its own local storage, on your device only: when you last closed the licence reminder, so that it does not appear too often, and, while you are signed in, your email address and its first letter, so that the account button can show them. These notes are not sent to us or anyone else. Signing out removes the account note, and clearing your browser's site data removes both.
  • Hosting and security. The Site and Accounts are hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes visitors' IP addresses and technical request data to deliver the Site and protect it from attacks. On sign-up and sign-in forms, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks browser and device signals to tell people from bots and does not require you to solve puzzles. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Fonts. The Site's fonts load from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google, which receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Google's privacy policy applies to that information.

3.2 When you email us

Email to us, including messages sent from the Requests and submissions page, is handled by Gmail (Google). We receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the contents and attachments of your message. We use this only to read and respond to you, review requests and submissions, and keep a record of our correspondence. Submissions are also governed by the Submission terms.

3.3 When you buy

Purchases go through Payhip, and payments are processed by Payhip and its payment processors, Stripe and PayPal. We never see your full card details. We receive the order information Payhip shares with sellers, such as your name, email address, the Product purchased, the price, and the location information needed for sales tax. We use it to deliver your purchase, provide support, verify and manage Business Licences, prevent fraud, and keep the records the law requires, including tax records.

3.4 When you create and use an Account

  • Account details: your email address and, if you choose to give it, your name. We use passwordless sign-in: we email you a one-time code or link. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of each code, and delete it once it expires. If we ever offer passwords, we will store only a salted hash, never the password itself.
  • Acceptance records: the date and time you accepted the Terms and the Licence, the versions you accepted, a keyed hash of the IP address used, and browser information. We keep these to prove the agreement and protect our rights.
  • Sign-in and session data: a strictly necessary cookie containing a random session identifier, plus records of sign-in times, keyed hashes of IP addresses, and browser type, used to keep you signed in and to detect misuse.
  • Download logs: which skill files and versions you download, when, and a keyed hash of the IP address used, used to provide downloads, prevent abuse and automated scraping, and enforce the Licence. We do not store your IP address itself. We store a keyed hash of it: a scrambled code made with a secret key that only LlmentZero holds. It lets us recognise repeated use from the same address to prevent abuse and enforce the Licence, but it cannot practically be turned back into the address without that key.
  • Licence records: Business Licences linked to your Account (see section 10A), including those matched automatically from Payhip purchase notifications that use the same email address.
  • Marketing: only if you tick the separate, optional box (see section 5).

4. Consent

We collect, use and disclose personal information only with your consent, except where the law permits or requires otherwise. By emailing us or making a purchase, you consent to the uses described above. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits (for example, we must keep tax records), by emailing llmentzero@gmail.com. We will explain the likely consequences of withdrawing.

5. Email marketing

We will not send you commercial electronic messages unless you have agreed to receive them or the law otherwise allows, in line with Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL). Every marketing email will identify LlmentZero and include a free, easy way to unsubscribe, which we will action within 10 business days.

6. Service providers and disclosure

6.1 We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, namely Cloudflare (hosting, Accounts, database, file delivery, security and bot protection), our transactional email provider (for example, Resend) for sign-in codes and account emails, GitHub (source code), Google (email and fonts), Payhip (store and checkout), and Stripe and PayPal (payment processing), and only as needed for the purposes in section 3.

6.2 We may also disclose personal information where required by law, or to a successor if the business is transferred (for example, to a future corporation), on the condition that it continues to be protected as described in this policy.

6.3 Some service providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While there, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities.

7. Safeguards

We protect personal information with safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity, including using reputable providers, strong passwords and two-factor authentication on our accounts, and limiting access to what is needed.

8. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes it was collected for, or longer where the law requires. Purchase and tax records are generally kept for six years, as the Canada Revenue Agency requires. Correspondence that is no longer needed is deleted. Account records are kept as follows: sign-in codes and expired sessions are deleted within a day; download logs and security logs (including keyed hashes of IP addresses) are kept for up to 12 months; acceptance records and licence records are kept for as long as the Account exists and then for six years, to prove agreements and meet legal requirements; if you close your Account, your profile is deleted within 30 days, except for records we must or may lawfully keep.

9. Your rights

You may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent. Email llmentzero@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days, as PIPEDA requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Privacy breaches

If a breach of security safeguards involving your personal information creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires.

10A. Licence records and identifiers

When you buy a Business Licence or other licence, we may assign it a licence ID and keep a record linking that ID to your order and your Account (name, email, Product, number of Devices and date). We use this only to verify and manage licences, answer licensing questions and enforce the Licence, and we keep it for as long as the licence is in effect plus the record-keeping period in section 8. Free skill files are the same for everyone and do not contain any personal information.

10B. Text and data mining reservation

LlmentZero reserves all rights in the Site and Skills against text and data mining and AI training, including through the W3C TDM Reservation Protocol (TDMRep) and our robots.txt file. This does not involve collecting any information about you.

10C. Information you give AI tools

When you use our Skills with an AI System, the information you provide to that system goes to its provider, not to LlmentZero. That provider's privacy policy applies. You are responsible for deciding what personal or confidential information you share with AI Systems.

11. Children

The Site and Products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

12. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version with a new date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use information we already hold, we will seek consent where required.
