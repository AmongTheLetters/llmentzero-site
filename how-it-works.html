Process

How it works

  1. Pick a skill or a bundle

    Start with one basic skill, or pick a bundle for a whole role or function.

  2. Give it to your AI

    Load the documents as project sources, folder files, or agent knowledge.

  3. Your AI checks itself

    Self-check tasks with model answers let the AI confirm it learned the skill.

  4. Combine and grow

    Skills compose: basics combine into compound skills, then into roles and full functions.

Structure

Inside every document

  • Table of contents with a one-line explanation per section
  • Decision rules
  • A sample of the finished output
  • Self-check tasks with model answers
  • A short quick-card version
