Tiers and licences

The tiers

Six levels, from a single skill to a complete function. Every skill is free for personal use.

One licence covers every tier

Business Licence: CA$29 per device

For work use, one Business Licence covers every LlmentZero skill in every tier. There is no separate tier or bundle pricing. You need one for each computer or device where a skill is used for work.

What each tier is

Tier 01

Basic

One simple skill. A single, focused skill an AI can learn and apply on its own.

Tier 02

Compound

A few basics combined. Several basic skills joined into one workflow.

Tier 03

General

A full role bundle. Everything for a whole role, such as project management.

Tier 04

Specialist

A focused add-on. Extends a role into a niche, such as SaaS.

Tier 05

Expert

Deep, advanced material. Advanced methods for hard, high-stakes work.

Tier 06

Complete

A whole function. Several specializations that together run a function, such as a marketing division.
